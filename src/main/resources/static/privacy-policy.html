Privacy Policy for Safarnama

Last updated: 7th September 2026

Safarnama ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and a securely hashed password when you create an account.
  • Travel content: countries, cities, and entries you create, including notes, ratings, and photos you add.
  • Location data: only when you search for or select a location to attach to an entry, or when you tap "Use current location." We do not track your location in the background.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To sync your travel data across your devices
  • To provide the features of the app (location search, entry creation)

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on servers hosted by Render. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app, under Profile > Delete Account.

Contact
